⚠ Competitiveness gap

  • $450/t GMB price > global $300–350/t
  • 30% premium vs import parity
  • Only viable with tariff protection
  • Reformed cost $398/t narrows gap
  • Proximity premium = 2 weeks vs 6 weeks sea

✗ Hidden costs inflate real price

  • GMB payment delays: 60–180 days
  • FX spread: official vs parallel rate
  • Irrigation electricity: subsidised, fragile
  • True cost likely $520–580/t
  • Export uncompetitive without reform

Site-visit questions (kill-or-confirm)

A. Production reality check

  • Actual production? Private vs official data?
  • GMB payment timeline? Days to receipt?
  • Side-selling volume? % sold outside GMB?
  • Irrigation cost? $/ha with grid vs solar?
  • Fertiliser cost? $/t in ZiG vs USD?

B. Export feasibility

  • Export licence process? How long? Who decides?
  • Beitbridge dwell time? For grain specifically?
  • Zambia/Moz buyer contact? Any existing deals?
  • Quality grading: What spec do buyers want?
  • Price at which Zambia buys?

C. Finance & investment climate

  • ZiG stability? Real depreciation rate?
  • Land tenure security? For new investment?
  • DFI appetite? Any existing ag credit lines?
  • Solar for irrigation? VAT deferment used?
  • Private power PPA examples operating?

D. Political economy

  • Export ban history? Triggers? Frequency?
  • GMB reform appetite? Private buyer channel?
  • ZMX adoption? Actual volumes traded?
  • Sovereignty narrative? Can export be framed positively?
  • Government champion — who? ARDAS? Ministry?
ABANDON ENTIRE VC IF 2+ TRUE:
  • Private-sector data confirms production <200kt (self-sufficiency not achieved)
  • GMB refuses any private/parallel buying channel for wheat
  • RBZ blocks USD finance to agriculture entirely
  • Export licensing remains opaque and discretionary with no reform path
  • Beitbridge green-lane blocked by both SA and Zim border authorities
